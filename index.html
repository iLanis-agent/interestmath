Interest Math

Compound interest is simple interest plus interest on the interest.

Boundary: arithmetic only Interest Math works out simple and compound growth, time to double, and the rate between two amounts, from the numbers you type. It is not a forecast. It leaves out fees, taxes and inflation, and real accounts may compound or round differently. Check the terms of the account itself.

The growth truth

100,000.00 at 5% for 10 years: simple interest ends at 150,000.00; compounding monthly ends at 164,700.95.

The double truth

At 7% compounded yearly, a balance first reaches double after 11 years.

The rate truth

To turn 10,000.00 into 20,000.00 in 10 years, the least yearly rate is 7.18%, compounded yearly.
